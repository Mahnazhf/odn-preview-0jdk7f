Privacy

This page describes what actually happens on this website — measured against the site's own code, not a generic template.

Controller

The controller responsible for data processing on this website is Oncodianova GmbH, Glückstraße 57, 22081 Hamburg, Germany — full contact details in the imprint.

No cookies, no tracking

This website does not set cookies, does not use browser local or session storage, and does not load any third-party analytics, advertising, or social-media scripts. Fonts are served from this website's own domain, not from a third-party font service. No visitor behaviour is tracked.

Server log files

Calling up this website causes technical connection data — for example IP address, date and time of access, browser type, and the page requested — to be processed automatically by our hosting provider, in order to deliver the page and keep it technically secure. This preview is currently served as a private, non-indexed staging environment for internal review. The hosting provider and log-retention period for the public production website had not been finally confirmed when this page was drafted and will be added here once decided.

Contact by e-mail

You may contact us by e-mail at info@oncodianova.com. Any personal data your message contains is used only to process and answer your enquiry.

Order enquiries to our distribution partner sent to order@biozol.de are copied to info@oncodianova.com — shown as "CC" directly next to the address wherever it appears on this website — so that we are informed of order requests. You may object to this at any time by contacting us.

External links and downloads

This website links to external scientific resources (for example PubMed, DOI, PMC, UniProt, NCBI Gene) and to our distribution partner and to CAL2® at cal2antibody.com. These are separate providers with their own privacy policies; we have no influence over their data processing. Datasheets, safety data sheets and posters offered for download are hosted on this website's own domain; downloading them does not send data to a third party.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, and restriction of processing of your personal data, and the right to object to processing. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular the authority responsible for our registered seat in Hamburg (Der Hamburgische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).
